# Medicine-Storage-PRJ_283-


A smart system that uses the internet of things 

to monitor medicine storage for temperature 

and humidity. 

Abstract— Medicine needs a constant required env to maintain their efficiency. Two such conditions 

for medicines that must be considered when taken out of range of their suitable limit is their 

temperature and humidity. Most of these checks require a human to actually do it, thus making it hard 

for any of the user to get constant status report at the end of the day. Through this project I'm trying 

to develop a smart medicine storage monitoring system that monitors temperature and humidity of a 

medicine storage area by the use of sensors. These values read by the sensors is feed to microcontroller 

and the reading display. If the storage temp and hum goes out of the limit specified then a alert system 

will notify user about that condition of medicine storage. The smart system is designed in a way to be 

cheap and easily manageable. This smart system can be used in home, in the dispensaries, in 

pharmacies and even in any other small medical facilities. At the end of this project, it has tried and 

designed and presented a solution to this ongoing problem of continuous monitoring of medicine 

storages to find out unfavorable conditions in earliest to save their efficiency and prevent its 

breakdown.
